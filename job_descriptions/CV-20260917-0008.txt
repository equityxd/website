Business Analyst – Greenfield ERP Project

Location: Brussels, Belgium
Working model: Hybrid (3 days on-site)
Contract: Contract / Freelance
Sector: Logistics / Transport

Role Overview
Our client, a leading organisation within the logistics and transport sector, is embarking on a Greenfield ERP transformation programme. We are seeking an experienced Business Analyst to support the programme from initial business needs assessment through to ERP selection and implementation.
The successful candidate will analyse current processes and systems, define business requirements, support the evaluation and selection of an ERP solution, and help establish the future-state operating model.

Key Responsibilities:

    Assess and document AS-IS processes, systems, data and interfaces across the business.
    Identify process gaps, inefficiencies, pain points and improvement opportunities.
    Lead workshops with business stakeholders and process owners to capture and prioritise requirements.
    Develop business, functional, non-functional, data and integration requirements.


ERP Selection

    Develop the ERP requirements catalogue and evaluation criteria.
    Support RFI/RFP, vendor selection and ERP demonstrations.
    Evaluate ERP solutions against business requirements and operational needs.
    Provide analysis to support the final ERP selection and implementation strategy.


Key Experience

    Experience as a Business Analyst within ERP transformation / implementation programmes.
    Strong process mapping, requirements gathering and fit-gap analysis experience.
    Strong stakeholder management and workshop facilitation skills.
    Experience working with ERP vendors and/or systems integrators.

NET ASSESSMENT (strategist view)
================================================================================
The candidate is a strong DATA/BI + BUSINESS ANALYST with clear, direct evidence on the two
core JD demands — data migration to a new platform (SAP S/4HANA) and functional business
analysis — and matches location, language, tenure, and toolset. The primary risks are
(1) the role is data-platform/functional rather than finance-centric, and (2) the preferred
Healthcare sector experience is absent. Recommend advancing to the 1-stage interview, with
prepared probing questions on enterprise data-platform depth, formal data-mapping/spec
artifacts, and any healthcare-domain exposure.

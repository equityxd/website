## Question 1 — Leading a large-scale transformation / ERP implementation

**"Tell me about a time you led a major transformation initiative — for example, an ERP migration — from diagnosis through go-live."**

- **Situation:** At **Engie SEM** (Freelance Strategic Business Analyst & Finance Automation Lead, Jan 2026 – Present, Brussels), the organization was migrating its global finance systems to **SAP S/4HANA** across French subsidiaries.
- **Task:** I was tasked with leading the **financial controlling pilot phase** and serving as the core **Business Analyst** bridging IT architecture and strategic business operations — a role requiring me to translate complex IT design into operational reality.
- **Action:** I designed, built, and deployed an automated budget-modeling tool from scratch within a tight **2-week timeline** to maintain operational continuity during a resource gap, replacing an unreliable legacy framework. I also streamlined financial-closing by deploying **PowerQuery ETL workflows** to safely ingest **1,500+ bookings per close with zero human intervention**, and reconciled middle-office (IVDB) data with SAP by restructuring the Work Breakdown Structure project codes to optimize portfolio monitoring.
- **Result:** The pilot ensured continuity during a resource gap, eliminated manual error in the close, and produced an optimized, monitorable project structure — directly de-risking the S/4HANA go-live and demonstrating how finance can be positioned as a growth enabler rather than a back-office function.

TASK
Assess and document the existing (AS-IS) processes, systems, data and interfaces across the business to establish a diagnosis before transformation — the JD's first responsibility.

ACTION
- Acted as the core Business Analyst bridging IT architecture and strategic business operations.
- Assessed and documented AS-IS processes, systems, data and interfaces between the middle-office (IVDB) database and SAP S/4HANA.
- Restructured and simplified Work Breakdown Structure (WDS) project codes to strengthen portfolio monitoring and reporting.

RESULT
- Established a clear AS-IS baseline linking the middle-office data to the ERP.
- Improved portfolio monitoring and reporting fidelity.
- Set the diagnosis that informed subsequent requirements and remediation.

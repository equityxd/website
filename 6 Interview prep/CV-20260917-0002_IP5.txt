Q: Describe a time you developed business, functional, data and integration requirements for a programme.

SITUATION
Engie Tractebel (Mar–Dec 2023), Cash Flow & Financial Modeling Specialist, project finance reporting atop SAP.

TASK
Define and structure requirements — business, functional, data and integration — to connect ERP data to business outcomes.

ACTION
- Defined cash-flow reporting (business/functional) and per-project cash-exposure (data) requirements for project finance.
- Modelled finance data from SAP and designed Power BI reporting to connect ERP data to actionable business insights (integration).
- Assessed subsidiary financing needs through credit analysis and impairment testing (business/compliance requirements).

RESULT
- Connected ERP data to decision-ready BI reporting.
- Established clear business and data requirements underpinning project-finance reporting.

RECRUITER NOTE / PROBE
- Strong on business/data/integration requirements; weaker on non-functional requirements — this is a documented gap.
- Probe: how non-functional requirements (performance, security, availability) were handled, and whether requirements were baselined/version-controlled with stakeholders.

Q. Give an example of improving an accounting process through automation.
SITUATION: Financial close was manual and error-prone.
TASK: Automate the close while keeping control intact.
ACTION: Built a PowerQuery ETL pipeline ingesting 1,500+ bookings per close with zero manual intervention.
RESULT: Faster, error-free closes — reusable accounting/automation experience for the new ERP's finance flows.

  The JD core qualification is "strong background in Business Intelligence / Analytics combined with Data Architecture or Data Governance." The candidate's career is finance/accounting/transformation, not dedicated data-architecture or data-governance ownership. SUMMARY
================================================================================
STRONG FIT DIMENSIONS: Power BI / enterprise BI tooling (Power BI, QlikSense, Business Object, Cognos, Hyperion), BI/analytics reporting delivery, multi-domain stakeholder & multi-country exposure, finance-data ETL/reporting, and a genuine EU-funded-programme (FP7) touchpoint.

PRIMARY MISALIGNMENT: The role is a dedicated BI/Data-Governance/Data-Architecture consultancy seat. The candidate is a Finance/Transformation leader whose BI and data work is applied within finance mandates, not a standalone data profession. The most material gaps are: no demonstrated formal data architecture/governance ownership, no conceptual→logical→physical data modelling evidence, and no exposure to the JD's specific stack (Microsoft Fabric, Data Lakehouse, data warehousing/dimensional modelling, Oracle/MySQL, cloud data platforms).

NET ASSESSMENT: Partial match. The candidate is a strong BI-reporting and stakeholder/transformation profile but does not, as documented, meet the core Data Architecture / Data Governance essential qualifications. Best positioned for BI/analytics reporting or finance-analytics adjacent mandates; not a confirmed fit for a lead data-architecture/governancy consultancy without validating the modelling and governance depth.

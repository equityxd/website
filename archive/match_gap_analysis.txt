   evaluation criteria," "Support RFI/RFP, vendor selection and ERP
   demonstrations," "Evaluate ERP solutions against business requirements."
   GAP: No evidence of RFI/RFP, vendor selection, ERP demos, or a requirements
   catalogue. His SAP exposure is implementation/migration, not selection.

2. WORKSHOP FACILITATION — JD: "Lead workshops with business stakeholders and
   process owners."
   GAP: No explicit evidence of LEADING workshops or facilitation; stakeholder
   mention is passive ("liaised with auditors").

3. VENDOR / SYSTEMS INTEGRATOR MANAGEMENT — JD: "Experience working with ERP
   vendors and/or systems integrators."
   GAP: No evidence of managing external ERP vendors or systems integrators.

4. NON-FUNCTIONAL & INTEGRATION REQUIREMENTS — JD: "Develop business,
   functional, non-functional, data and integration requirements."
   GAP: Non-functional requirements are not evidenced; interface/data work is
   present but framed as reconciliation/ETL, not requirements engineering.

5. CORE TITLE / PERMANENT BA ROLE — JD: "Experience as a Business Analyst
   within ERP transformation / implementation programmes."
   NOTE: The candidate's BA experience is in FREELANCE / consultancy engagements,
   and his primary career identity is "Finance leader / CFO / transformation
   executive." The current BA title is attached to a "Finance Automation Lead"
   mandate. This is a positioning gap rather than a hard skills gap.

6. SECTOR — JD sector is Logistics / Transport.
   GAP: Candidate's background is Finance/Accounting/Real Estate/Airports — no
   logistics or transport sector exposure is evident.

7. EDUCATION / QUALIFICATIONS — JD lists qualifications as "Key Experience."
   Note: ESCP (Auditing & Consulting) + Master's in Financial Control. Strong
   fit for a finance-oriented BA, but no dedicated Business Analyst
   certification/qualification is listed.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERALL ASSESSMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fit: PARTIAL / STRONG ON TECHNIQUES, WEAK ON SELECTION & FACILITATION.

The candidate is a credible ERP-transformation Business Analyst with real
SAP S/4HANA migration experience and solid process/data/requirements skills.
He is a strong match on the "AS-IS diagnosis," "process mapping," "fit-gap
analysis," and "requirements gathering" pillars of the JD.

However, the role is a GREENFIELD ERP SELECTION mandate, and the candidate's
evidence is weighted toward IMPLEMENTATION/MIGRATION and FINANCE CONTROL, not
the selection, RFI/RFP, vendor evaluation, and workshop-facilitation pillars
the JD emphasizes. The strongest gap is the absence of explicit ERP selection,
RFP, and standalone workshop-facilitation experience.

Recommended next-step questions for the recruiter:
- Does the mandate emphasize ERP selection (RFP/vendor) or implementation —
  and which does the candidate's profile best serve?
- Can workshop facilitation and vendor/integrator be demonstrated via
  consultative engagements not fully captured on the CV?
- Is logistics/transport sector experience a hard requirement or a plus?
